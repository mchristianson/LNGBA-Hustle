How it works

Parents

  1. You get an email with a link for your player.
  2. Tap it. The app asks "Is this you?" Tap Yes.
  3. See your team's schedule.
  4. At the game, tap a button each time a hustle play happens.
  5. Tapped the wrong one? Hit Undo.

Coaches

  1. Get your own login link.
  2. Add a game in seconds: opponent, date, time, place.
  3. See results from past games and the season leaderboard.
  4. Score players yourself if you want.
  5. Snap a photo of the game's top hustle player for social media.

The association

  1. Send one spreadsheet: players, jersey numbers, teams, parent and coach emails.
  2. We load it. Everyone gets their link.
  3. Each team sees a live leaderboard all season.
  4. At season end, you get the top player on each team, with photos, for trophies and posts.

Lost the email? Go to the site, type your email, and the app sends a new login link. Points save even with weak gym Wi-Fi and upload when the signal comes back.
