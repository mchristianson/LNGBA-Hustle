# LNGBA Hustle Tracker: How It Works

A simple phone app for tracking hustle points at every travel game. No passwords. No spreadsheets.

## For parents

1. You get an email: "Start tracking hustle points for Emma #12."
2. Tap the link. The app asks: "Is this you?" and shows your name and your player. Tap **Yes**.
3. You see your team's schedule.
4. At the game, open the game and tap buttons as plays happen. Each player has a button for each hustle play: Rebound +1, Steal +1, Block +1, Deflection +1, Charge +3, Assist +2, Turnover -2.
5. Totals update right away. Tapped the wrong button? Hit **Undo**.
6. Points save even with weak gym Wi-Fi. They upload when your signal comes back.

Lost the email? Go to the site, type your email, and we send you a new login link.

## For coaches

1. You get your own login link.
2. Add games in a few seconds: opponent, date, time, location.
3. See results from every past game and the season leaderboard.
4. Score players yourself if you want.
5. After a game, snap a photo of the top hustle player. It is saved with the game for social media later.

## For the association

1. Send us one spreadsheet: players, jersey numbers, teams, parent emails, coach emails.
2. We load it, and the app emails everyone their link.
3. Every team sees its own live leaderboard all season.
4. At season end, you get a list of the top hustle player on each team, with photos, ready for trophies and social posts.

## Questions for you

1. One parent scores each game today. Should we keep one scorer per game? (We recommend yes, so points are not counted twice.)
2. Should the trophy go to the most total points, or the best points per game?
3. Do you have photo releases for all players?
4. Should parents see only their own team, or all teams?
